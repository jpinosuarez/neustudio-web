# NeuStudio - Project Brief & Business Objectives

## Mission
NeuStudio is a boutique Web-as-a-Service (WaaS) agency operating directly in Berlin. We deliver hyper-optimized, high-converting digital platforms for local businesses, freelancers, and the vibrant expat community. We provide a completely managed, frictionless technological partnership based on recurring subscriptions.

## Core Philosophy: "Silent Tech"
We focus on business results, lead generation, and conversion rates, NOT technical jargon. The client doesn't need to know what Astro or Tailwind is; they only need to know their website is the fastest in Berlin, requires zero maintenance from them, and is completely legally compliant.

## Value Proposition
- **Extreme Performance:** Instant load times (100/100 Core Web Vitals) to maximize conversion rates and dominate local SEO.
- **Zero Friction:** Clients pay a predictable monthly fee. We handle hosting, maintenance, and technical updates.
- **Absolute Trust (DSGVO):** 100% DSGVO/GDPR compliance out of the box, with zero non-consented tracking.

## Target Audience
Local Berlin commerce and expats who need a bilingual or trilingual (ES/EN/DE) digital presence without the technical headache of managing legacy CMS platforms.

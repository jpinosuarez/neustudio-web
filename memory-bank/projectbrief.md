# NeuStudio - Project Brief & Business Objectives

## 1. Mission & Positioning
NeuStudio is a modern, agile boutique Web-as-a-Service (WaaS) agency operating directly in Berlin[cite: 12]. We distance ourselves from unreliable freelancers by projecting the solidity of a European agency combined with empathetic, accessible communication[cite: 12]. We deliver hyper-optimized, high-converting digital platforms for local businesses and the vibrant expat community[cite: 13].

## 2. Target Audience & Emotional Goal
- **Audience:** Local Berlin commerce (clinics, restaurants, studios), independent professionals, and expat entrepreneurs[cite: 10].
- **Pain Points:** Stressed, tight initial budgets, or frustrated by slow legacy websites that fail to attract clients[cite: 10].
- **Emotional Goal:** The client must feel immediate **relief**[cite: 10]. They need to understand that delegating their web presence will not be a technical or financial headache[cite: 10]. We provide peace of mind by showing there is a real, empathetic human team behind the service[cite: 10].

## 3. Brand Personality & Copywriting Guidelines
- **Personality:** Minimalist, Bold, and Creative[cite: 10]. Friendly, empathetic, and highly authoritative in business outcomes[cite: 12].
- **No Technical Jargon:** NEVER use terms like "JAMstack", "Core Web Vitals", or "Serverless" facing the client[cite: 11]. 
  - *Bad:* "We build static sites in Astro with 100ms TTFB."[cite: 11]
  - *Good:* "We build ultra-fast websites so your clients never wait, and Google rewards you for it."[cite: 11]
- **Relief-Focused Copy:** Emphasize that we handle everything[cite: 11]. *Example:* "You focus on serving your clients; we make sure your phone keeps ringing."[cite: 11]
- **Price Transparency:** Position our service as a low-risk, predictable flat-rate monthly investment[cite: 11].

## 4. Business Model (Dual-Pricing WaaS)
We sell client acquisition solutions packaged as continuous services, shifting CapEx to predictable OpEx[cite: 12].
- **Tier 1 (Web One-Shot):** Accessible setup fee + minor monthly subscription[cite: 12, 13]. Client owns the asset[cite: 13].
- **Tier 2 (Socio WaaS):** Zero/low setup fee + premium monthly retainer with a strict 12-month contract lock-in to amortize development hours[cite: 12, 13].
- **Sales Strategy:** Consultative selling[cite: 12]. Direct "Buy Now" checkout buttons are strictly forbidden[cite: 12]. Clients must pass through a strict qualification form before booking a call[cite: 12].

# NeuStudio - Project Brief & Business Objectives

## Mission
NeuStudio is a boutique Web-as-a-Service (WaaS) agency operating directly in Berlin. We deliver hyper-optimized, high-converting digital platforms for local businesses, freelancers, and the vibrant expat community. We provide a completely managed, frictionless technological partnership.

## Core Philosophy: "Silent Tech"
We focus strictly on business results, lead generation, and conversion rates, NOT technical jargon. The client doesn't need to know what Astro, JAMstack, or Tailwind is. UI copy must always highlight business value (e.g., "Speed that Sells" instead of "Lightweight Architecture"). 

## Dual-Pricing Business Model
We offer a segmented pricing strategy to capture both traditional clients and scale-focused businesses:
1. **Tier 1 (Web Propietaria / Ownership):** High initial setup fee (e.g., 349€) + low monthly maintenance (49€/mo). Client owns the asset. Includes hosting, security, and minor text/image updates.
2. **Tier 2 (Socio WaaS / All-in-One):** Zero initial setup (0€) + premium monthly retainer (149€/mo) with a 12-month contract. Includes evolutionary maintenance, annual conversion audits, and advanced analytics.

## Target Audience
Local Berlin commerce (clinics, restaurants, studios) and expats who need a bilingual or trilingual (ES/EN/DE) digital presence that strictly complies with the German DSGVO without the technical headache of managing legacy CMS platforms.

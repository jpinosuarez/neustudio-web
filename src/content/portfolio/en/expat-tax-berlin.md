## The Challenge

ExpatTax was spending heavily on Google Ads but seeing a €45 CPA with abysmal landing page conversion. Their single-language WordPress site served German content only — alienating the English and Spanish-speaking expat communities that make up 40% of Berlin's workforce.

## Our Approach

We built a fully trilingual Astro SSG site (ES/EN/DE) with `[lang]` routing, each version with locally-optimized content and keyword strategy. The lead form was a native HTML form with Netlify Forms backend — zero JavaScript required. Schema.org `Service` and `ProfessionalService` structured data earned rich results in SERPs.

## The Results

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| CPA | €45 | €18 |
| Organic Sessions/Month | 320 | 1,216 |
| Pages Per Session | 1.2 | 4.7 |
| Bounce Rate | 78% | 32% |

> "Our expat clients finally feel understood when they land on the site and can read about Steuererklärung in their own language. The leads are higher quality too." — Ana Schmidt, Managing Director

## The Challenge

KüchenWelt's legacy WooCommerce site loaded in over 4 seconds and had a 1.2% conversion rate. Mobile users accounted for 70% of traffic, yet the experience was broken on small screens. Competitors like Amazon were siphoning sales daily.

## Our Approach

We migrated to a fully static Astro architecture with dynamic product filtering via URL search params — zero client-side JavaScript. The entire catalog was pre-rendered at build time, and we integrated structured data (Product schema, LocalBusiness schema) to dominate Berlin-local search results.

## The Results

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Page Load | 4.2s | 0.4s |
| Conversion Rate | 1.2% | 4.8% |
| Mobile Traffic Share | 70% | 82% |
| Revenue (6mo) | Baseline | +340% |
